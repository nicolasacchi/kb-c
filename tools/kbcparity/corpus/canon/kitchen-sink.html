Disclosure

The humble <details> tag, much-maligned, does in twelve characters what an accordion library does in sixty kilobytes.

What is the borrow checker?

A static analysis pass that enforces three rules at every program point: at most one &mut reference, no aliasing of &mut with &, and all references must be valid for their entire scope.

Why does Pin exist?

Self-referential structs — like the state machines async functions compile into — can’t be moved without invalidating the references they hold. Pin is a marker that says “don’t move me”.

What does ? actually do?

It’s sugar for: if Ok(x), evaluate to x; if Err(e), return Err(e.into()) from the enclosing function. The .into() is what makes thiserror’s #[from] work.
